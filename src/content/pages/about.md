**Fullstack Weekly** is a Sunday newsletter by Khai Nguyen — a fullstack engineer who reads too many tech blogs so you don't have to.

Every week I curate a handful of links on what's worth knowing in AI and tech. One story for each day of the week that just passed, with a short take on why it matters.
